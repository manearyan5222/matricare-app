## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 📅 Dual-Mode Timeline & Growth Engine
- **Pregnancy Mode (0–9 Months / Weeks 1–40)**:
  - Week-by-week fetal growth tracker using familiar Indian food/fruit size comparisons (e.g., Mustard seed 🟤, Amla 🍋, Guava 🍏, Banana 🍌, Green Coconut 🥥, Papaya 🍈, Watermelon 🍉).
  - Trimester indicators, maternal physiological changes, and clinical scan schedules (NT scan, TIFFA Anomaly scan, OGTT glucose test).
- **Postpartum & Toddler Mode (0–3 Years / Months 1–36)**:
  - Growth milestones (motor skills, speech, WHO height/weight markers).
  - Complete **IAP (Indian Academy of Pediatrics)** immunization checklist with interactive checkmarks.

### 2. 🧘‍♀️ Prenatal Yogasana & Baby Motor Play
- **For Expecting Mothers**: Butterfly Pose (*Baddha Konasana*), Cat-Cow Stretch (*Marjaryasana-Bitilasana*), Prenatal Pelvic Floor (Kegel Drills), and *Anulom Vilom* alternate nostril breathing.
- **For Baby & Postpartum**: Tummy Time Explorer (0–6m), Bicycle Legs Gas Relief (0–6m), and Reach & Grasp Arm Stretch.
- **Gamified Rewards**: Mark exercises complete to earn **⭐ Health Stars**!

### 3. 🍎 Daily Micronutrient Level Tracker
- Interactive level bars and food logging for:
  - 🥬 **Iron & Hemoglobin**: Spinach, Jaggery, Beetroot, Rajma.
  - 🥛 **Calcium & Bone Shield**: Curd (Dahi), Paneer, Milk, Til (Sesame) seeds.
  - 🥜 **Folic Acid & Cell Growth**: Moong Sprouts, Dal, Methi, Peanuts.
  - 🥚 **Protein & Muscle Power**: Eggs, Chana, Sprouts, Paneer.

### 4. 💡 Scientific Myth-Busting Module (Indian Context)
- Filterable Fact vs. Myth cards addressing traditional practices with WHO & IAP evidence:
  - Debunking *saffron milk for baby skin fairness* (genetics determines pigmentation).
  - Debunking *eating for two* (requires only +300 kcal/day extra nutrient-dense food).
  - Debunking *drinking ghee in 9th month for birth canal lubrication*.
  - Warning against *vigorous dough massages, nose pulling, and putting mustard oil in ears/eyes/nose*.
  - Promoting immediate *colostrum feeding* and *6 months exclusive breastfeeding* (debunking Janam Ghutti / honey).
  - Warning against *kajal (kohl)* lead toxicity in infant eyes.

### 5. 🩺 Symptom Checker & Red-Flag Protocols
- Vetted first-line home care steps for mild conditions (morning sickness, infant colic, diaper rash).
- **Pulsing Emergency Red-Flag Alert**: Triggered instantly for severe symptoms (e.g., vaginal bleeding, severe swelling/headache, decreased fetal movement, infant high fever <3 months, respiratory distress) with direct 102 national emergency dialer.

### 6. 💧 Hydration Log & Push Reminders
- Interactive 8-glass water counter (target 2.5–3L/day).
- Browser `Notification` API integration for daily prenatal supplement reminders.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Markup**: Semantic HTML5
- **Styling**: CSS3 (Custom Properties, Flexbox, Grid, Keyframe Animations, Glassmorphism)
- **Logic & State**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), LocalStorage API (zero backend server required)
- **Deployment**: GitHub Pages (Google CDN)

## 📜 Medical Compliance Statement

All advice, workout guidelines, milestone trackers, and diet matrices provided in **MatriCare** are strictly grounded in published clinical guidelines from the **World Health Organization (WHO)** and the **Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP)**.
